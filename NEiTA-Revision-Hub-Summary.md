# The Year 12 ATAR MPA Revision Hub — A Summary of Teaching Practice
### Prepared to inform a National Excellence in Teaching Awards (NEiTA) nomination
**David Clarke · Media Production & Analysis · Sacred Heart College · 2026**

---

## Overview

The Revision Hub is a purpose-built, self-authored web platform that supports Year 12 ATAR Media Production & Analysis (MPA) students through the most demanding year of their schooling. Rather than distributing static notes, the teacher designed an integrated suite of **interactive, self-paced learning tools** — answer guides, essay walkthroughs, a strategy guide with exam simulation, a forensic post-exam feedback report, a past-paper bank, and subject-themed revision games — all hosted in one consistent, professionally designed environment mapped directly to the WA SCSA Unit 3 and Unit 4 syllabus. It demonstrates initiative, technical skill, deep subject knowledge, and an unwavering focus on student growth.

## Innovative, student-centred design

Every resource is built around how students actually learn under pressure, not merely what they need to know. The platform models a **"write first, then compare"** philosophy: model answers, exemplars and marking guidance are deliberately hidden behind a click so students attempt a response before seeing the standard. Tools include retrieval-practice flashcards, expandable concept libraries, and a "build-it-yourself" **Scene Playbook** in which students assemble and print their own analytical evidence — reflecting the teacher's belief that *"the strongest revision tool is the one you build yourself."*

## Feedback that builds independent, reflective learners

Feedback is the platform's standout strength. The teacher transforms a marked Semester One examination into a **"Cohort Feedback & Recalibration"** report: question-by-question breakdowns, quantified cohort trends, named common errors, and a bank of **real, anonymised student exemplars** annotated with the precise "move" that earned each mark. Students self-mark against authentic SCSA criteria using live marking rubrics, tiered exemplars showing what 12/12 versus 8/12 versus 3/12 looks like, and "what's lifting this / what's limiting this" diagnostics. The consistent message — *"This exam is a checkpoint, not a verdict"* — builds resilience alongside skill.

## Differentiation and inclusion

The platform meets a wide spread of abilities within one cohort. It offers explicit pathways for students "moving from a C to a B, a B to an A, or an A to something higher," vocabulary "upgrade" tables, sentence-level scaffolds, and extension material for ambitious students. Choice is built in: students may substitute their own set texts, with dedicated "pivot" guidance provided for alternative films. Multiple modalities — read, self-quiz, flashcard, and **AI-assisted podcast audio** for those who prefer to listen — ensure every learner has an entry point.

## Authentic assessment alignment

Content is rigorously aligned to the real examination: authentic command-word pedagogy (Construct / Describe / Analyse / Evaluate), true-to-exam mark weightings, eight years of past WACE and SCSA papers, and **timed "mock mode"** that simulates Section One (15 minutes) and Section Two (60 minutes) conditions, including a plan-and-draft split. Students rehearse not just content but exam temperament.

## Engagement, motivation and wellbeing

A site-wide countdown to the major Art Film submission keeps students focused, while subject-themed **brain-break games** — a media-terminology word game, a "Connections"-style concept-categorisation puzzle, and an arcade runner threaded with director quotes — make revision genuinely enjoyable and reinforce terminology through play. Throughout, a warm personal teacher voice ("A message from Mr Clarke") sustains relationship and care, including resources designed to keep learning moving on days the teacher is absent.

## Accessibility, equity and professional craft

The platform is responsive across devices, keyboard- and screen-reader-aware, print-friendly, and saves each student's progress on their own device. It sits behind a single secure login so resources can be shared equitably and safely with the class. The build quality — consistent design system, considered typography, robust interactivity — reflects significant personal investment of professional skill and time well beyond expectation.

## Impact and reflective practice

The Hub is explicitly a **living resource**, updated across the year as new assessments are marked and new needs emerge, and grounded in the teacher's analysis of genuine cohort data. It exemplifies reflective, evidence-informed teaching: identifying where students lost marks, building targeted tools to close those gaps, and returning the learning to students in a form they can act on. The teacher's own framing captures the intent: *"history is firmly on your side: this cohort almost always finishes far stronger than it starts."*
