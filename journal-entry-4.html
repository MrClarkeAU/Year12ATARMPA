A message from Mr Clarke
This is your roadmap for Journal Entry 4

This response is the most conceptually demanding extended task you've sat so far. The shift from Journal Entry 3 is real — you're being asked to think about representations as plural and interpretable, not just constructed by a director. Spirited Away rewards that thinking richly, but the work has to come from you: name the readings, weigh them, and make a judgement about how the film's aesthetics open them up. You'll see exemplar paragraphs from Imogen and Taylor scattered through this guide — these are real responses from your cohort, included with permission, that show what the top band looks like in practice. – Mr Clarke
